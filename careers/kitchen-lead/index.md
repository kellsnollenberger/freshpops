FRESH POPS is a neighborhood bar and listening room opening in Bed-Stuy, Brooklyn. We’re hiring a kitchen lead to help get the kitchen open and run day-to-day service. The role is full-time, 40+ hours a week, with some part-time hours possible before opening.

## The Role

You’ll lead prep and service, manage inventory and ordering, and help plan the kitchen schedule. You’ll also keep recipes, per-portion costs, and allergen sheets current for every dish and special, and communicate changes to the front-of-house team.
Once we’re open, there’ll be room to bring your own ideas to the kitchen and work with us to develop specials and new menu items.


## Availability and Certification

You’ll need availability for nights, weekends, holidays, and eventually brunch. A NYC Food Protection Certificate is required. If you don’t have one yet, you’ll need to get it promptly.

## How to Apply

To apply, email your résumé and a short note about your kitchen experience and availability to [hiring@freshpops.nyc](mailto:hiring@freshpops.nyc).

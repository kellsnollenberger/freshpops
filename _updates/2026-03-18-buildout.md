We've been a little quiet over here, but a lot is happening behind the scenes.

The short version: the bar is real, and it's getting closer.

We've been spending time in the space, measuring, sketching, reworking ideas, and getting all the small details right before anything is built. It still looks like a raw room, but it's starting to feel like something.

We're also beginning to gather the pieces that will shape how it actually works:

- What it sounds like
- How it feels to sit in the room
- Where the bar lands
- How close you are to the music

Some of this is practical, some of it is instinct. A lot of it is trial and error.

In the meantime, we're working on things you won't see right away: menus, drinks, music programming, and the overall rhythm of the place once the doors are open.

No opening date yet. But we're moving.

More soon.
